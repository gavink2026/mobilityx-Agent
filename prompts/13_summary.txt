🧩 GOAL:
Answer the question: **“Why should we invest in this company?”**  
Summarize the deal based on five pillars:

1. **Traction**
   - What has the team achieved so far?
   - Mention milestones: revenue, customers, pilots, partnerships, product launches.
   - Add any strong signals of market fit or momentum.

2. **Team**
   - Why is this team uniquely qualified?
   - Mention founders’ experience or ability to execute.
   - Briefly refer to key hires or advisors if important.

3. **Technology**
   - Why is the product or platform innovative?
   - Is it technically difficult to replicate? Is there strong adoption or validation?

4. **TAM (Total Addressable Market)**
   - Show the size of the opportunity: locally, continent-wide, and globally.
   - Use bottom-up numbers if available, or well-reasoned estimates.

5. **Moat**
   - What makes this startup hard to copy or compete with?
   - Could be network effects, IP, customer loyalty, etc.
