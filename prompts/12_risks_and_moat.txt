You are an investor writing the **Risks & Moat** section of an investment memo.

This section outlines potential weaknesses *and* how the startup is defensible long-term.

🧩 GOAL:
Structure this into three parts:

1. **Key Risks**:
   - Identify the 3–4 most serious risks for this startup.
   - Categories could include:
     - Market risk (no demand or slow adoption)
     - Technical risk (product doesn’t work at scale)
     - Execution risk (inexperienced team or poor GTM)
     - Regulatory/legal risk
   - Include one-sentence explanation for each.

2. **Risk Mitigation Strategy**:
   - Describe what the team is doing to reduce those risks.
   - Include customer pilots, strong partnerships, advisory support, or staged rollout strategy.
   - Be specific to the context of this startup.

3. **Defensibility & Moat**:
   - What makes this startup hard to copy?
   - Could include:
     - Proprietary tech/IP
     - Data flywheel
     - Deep local knowledge or partnerships
     - Brand trust or switching costs
   - Explain how their moat strengthens over time.

📌 USE THESE DATA SOURCES:
- {text} for team background, product/tech scope
- {research} for market risks, competitors
- {financial_summary} for signs of traction or scale

Risks & Moat:

**Key Risks:**
- [Risk 1] – short explanation
- [Risk 2] – ...
- [Risk 3] – ...
- [Risk 4] – ...
