How to approach competition
Summary

Addressing competition in startup pitch decks is crucial; ignoring it or making exaggerated claims can hurt credibility.

Highlights
📈 Competition is inevitable and can drive innovation.
❌ Ignoring competition signals lack of demand or research.
📊 Cherry-picking competitors can mislead investors.
🏆 Avoid exaggerated claims about superiority to maintain credibility.
🔍 Identify specific gaps in the market your startup will fill.
📉 Presenting a comprehensive market view is more effective.
🗺️ Simplifying competitive positioning helps investors understand your place.
Key Insights
🌊 Embrace Competition: Viewing competition positively can inspire improvements and drive progress in your startup. Understanding this dynamic is essential for growth.
📚 Do Your Homework: Not addressing competition can imply that you haven’t researched the market thoroughly, which can deter potential investors.
🧩 Avoid Cherry-Picking: Highlighting selective features of competitors can create a biased view; provide a holistic perspective to build credibility.
🚫 Credibility is Key: Making bold claims about being better than industry giants can undermine your pitch. Aim for balanced comparisons to maintain investor trust.
🎯 Identify Market Gaps: Focus on specific needs unmet by competitors; this shows strategic thinking and a clear market opportunity.
📊 Comprehensive Market View: Use visual tools like charts to present an objective analysis of the competitive landscape, enhancing clarity for investors.
📏 Simplify Positioning: Clear and straightforward positioning against competitors makes it easier for investors to grasp your unique value proposition and market role.
